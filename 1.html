B2+ deep-dive — the complete outcome space of the opaque-mint download

Honest framing: your negative results across the B-lab and J-lab are the architecturally-correct answer — a trusted-domain string cannot enter the download pipeline from page code on a conforming engine. What this page does instead is enumerate the entire outcome space of B2: six variants covering every reachable combination of {blob-URL origin segment} × {From line}. The complete space is segment ∈ {null, your-own-origin} × From ∈ {hidden (auto), your-origin (gesture)}. trusteddomain.com is not in either dimension — that is the proof, and it is final. B2a/B2b/B2c establish the 2×2 matrix; B2d (nested opaque) and B2e (your original V4 blob-popup path) prove nesting and popups add nothing; B2f shows the filename is the only page-controlled string in a row (UI-salience test for your engine). Inert .txt probes. For your engine these are CI assertions: any outcome outside this matrix = an engine bug to FIX, not a technique to use.

B2a — OPAQUE · AUTO (your finding, cell 1)

Sandboxed iframe without allow-same-origin, postMessage-triggered download. EXPECT: From hidden + copy-link blob:null/<uuid>. The (null, hidden) cell.

B2b — SAME-ORIGIN SANDBOX · AUTO (the ceiling, cell 2)

sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-downloads", auto download. EXPECT: From hidden + copy-link blob:<your-origin>/<uuid>. Your own origin is the CEILING of the segment — the maximum string the browser will ever place there. A foreign domain is unreachable.

B2c — SAME-ORIGIN SANDBOX · GESTURE (cell 3)

Same frame as B2b but you click the button inside it. EXPECT: From = your origin + blob:<your-origin>/<uuid>. The fully-attributed cell. Arm, then click inside the frame.

B2d — NESTED OPAQUE (srcdoc in srcdoc, relayed)

Opaque sandboxed iframe containing another opaque sandboxed iframe (message-relayed both ways). EXPECT: still blob:null/<uuid> — nesting depth adds nothing to the segment.

B2e — OPAQUE BLOB-POPUP (your original V4 path)

Opaque iframe mints a blob:null/… HTML page and opens it as a popup during an in-frame gesture; the popup self-downloads its own blob. EXPECT: still blob:null/<uuid>, From hidden. Arm, then click inside the frame.

B2f — FILENAME CARRIER (UI-salience test)

Sets the download attribute to a trusted-looking filename (TRUSTED_report.txt). EXPECT: only the filename changes; From and URL are untouched. Your engine must render the filename as a filename — never prominent enough to be read as provenance.
